📐 ONEFORM // Technical Specifications & CMF Architecture

1. Dimensional Architecture

  • Carry-On Mode: 550 mm (H) × 380 mm (W) × 230 mm (D)
  • Deployed Desk Height: 735 mm (ISO 9241 Ergonomic Standard)
  • Work Surface Span: 640 mm (W) × 480 mm (D) Bi-Fold Leaf
  • Packing Cavity: 38 Liters with padded 16" laptop compartment
  • Tare Weight: 4.18 kg (Carbon-reinforced frame)

2. Mechanical Intelligence

  • Elevation Mechanism: Synchronized 4-Bar Pantograph Linkage
  • Kinematic Material: CNC-Machined Extruded 6061-T6 Aircraft Aluminum
  • Counterbalance: 120N Nitrogen Gas-Spring Hydraulic Strut
  • User Effort Required: < 4.5 N (< 0.5 kg single-handed lift)
  • Locking Mechanism: Over-Center Geometric Toggle Detent
  • Max Edge Load: 40 kg static load rating on front cantilever

  • Linkages: Dark Gunmetal Brushed Anodized Metal
  • Accent Trim: Deep Maroon Silicone Gasket Seal (Pantone 19-1528)
  • Wheels: 360° Silent Dual-Caster Swivel with Nitrile Rubber Treads
